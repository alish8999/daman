STRONG POINTS — PROMINENT (use 4–6 with icons or numbered badges)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. يعمل بدون إنترنت | Works Offline
     Full system runs locally on the cashier PC — no cloud subscription,
     no monthly internet dependency. Your data stays on your machine.

  2. تعدد العملات | Multi-Currency
     Sell in USD, New Syrian Pound (SYP), and Old Syrian Pound (SYP_OLD).
     Every transaction stores the exchange rate at that moment — reports
     stay accurate even when rates change later.

  3. رخصة مقفلة على الجهاز | Hardware-Locked License
     One-time purchase. License tied to the device — no recurring SaaS fees.

  4. واجهة عربية RTL | Arabic-First RTL Interface
     Built for Arab cashiers from day one. Optional English toggle.

  5. تسوية وردية شفافة | Transparent Shift Reconciliation
     Open/close shifts with starting, expected and actual cash.
     Every sale, return, payment and expense is tied to a shift.

  6. ترقية بدون فقدان بيانات | Upgrade Without Data Loss
     Start with Basic, upgrade to Pro or Ultimate anytime — all history kept.


FULL FEATURE LIST (group into columns or sections on poster)
-------------------------------------------------------------

  ── POINT OF SALE / نقطة البيع ──
  • Fast sales cart with barcode scanning
  • Multi-packaging SKUs (piece / box / carton) with separate prices
  • Reverse-math entry ("give me 500 SYP of rice")
  • Open / misc items and fractional quantities
  • Sales returns and PDF invoices
  • Supervisor PIN protection on sensitive actions

  ── INVENTORY / المخزون ──
  • Real-time stock tracking and low-stock alerts
  • Auto barcode generation and label printing
  • Permanent audit trail for every stock change
  • Spoilage / wastage recording (inventory loss — does NOT leave cash drawer)
  • Batch stocktake — enter actual counts vs system quantities in bulk
  • Bulk category price update — raise/lower entire category by % or fixed amount,
    with separate adjustments per currency group

  ── PURCHASES & SUPPLIERS / المشتريات والموردين ──
  • Record purchase invoices and update product costs
  • Update per-packaging sell prices on purchase
  • Supplier ledger — pay cash or on account, track balances

  ── CLIENTS & FINANCE / العملاء والمالية ──
  • Client ledger — credit sales and incoming payments
  • Account statement report (sales, returns, payments for any period)
  • Multi-currency checkout with FX rate snapshotted per transaction
  • Fixed local-currency pricing (e.g. MTN / Syriatel cards priced in SYP
    while the store base currency is USD)
  • Shift management and end-of-day cash reconciliation
  • Operating and personal expense tracking
  • Profit dashboard, P&L reports including wastage cost

  ── REPORTS / التقارير ──
  • Daily and weekly profit with margin percentage
  • Top-selling products and 7-day revenue chart
  • Per-product Item Ledger — full movement history
  • PDF export for reports and invoices

  ── SECURITY & BRANDING / الأمان والتخصيص ──
  • Secure login (JWT), role-based access (Admin / User)
  • Hardware-locked license
  • White-label branding: your logo, colours and store name (Ultimate plan)
  • Arabic + English bilingual interface
